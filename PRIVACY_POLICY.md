# Privacy Policy — Seclusa Calendar

**Effective: 2026-11-02**

Seclusa Calendar is a static progressive web app. It is provided as-is at no cost.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app does not create accounts, send analytics, set tracking cookies, or upload your calendar events. Data stays in this browser (localStorage).

Optional reminder notifications require your explicit permission in the browser. Backups are written only to files you choose to download; nothing is sent to a server.

The service worker may revalidate same-origin app shell files when you are online. It does not transmit event contents.
